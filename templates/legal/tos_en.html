1. What this platform is

The platform is built for programming contests and classes at Osh State University. It has an archive of problems with automatic checking, contests, an online compiler, a typing trainer, a live Quiz and online courses. It is free to use.

It is a university learning project. It has no company details or licences of its own, and its certificates confirm that you completed a course on the platform; they are not an official education document.

2. Your account

  • To register you only need a username and a password. An email address is optional and is used to recover your password.
  • One person, one account. Do not share your password: everything done from your account is treated as done by you.
  • Do not register under someone else's name or choose a username that impersonates or insults someone.
  • Some features work without an account: reading problems and courses, the compiler, and joining a Quiz game with a game code.

3. Contests and problems

  • Solve problems on your own. During a contest do not use other people's solutions, share your solutions, help other users or sign in to someone else's account.
  • In contests with protection enabled, the platform records switches to other tabs or windows and text pasted into the editor. This is not a verdict by itself, but organisers can see these records and may remove points, cancel a result or remove a user from the contest.
  • Do not submit code that tries to escape the sandbox, read other people's data, reach the network, disrupt the judging system or create excessive load.
  • The contest organisers' decision on disputed questions is final.

4. Compiler

  • The compiler is open to everyone, including visitors without an account. Run time and resources are limited.
  • Do not run mining, network attacks, password crackers or programs that overload the server.
  • Files and projects are stored reliably only in an account. A guest's code can disappear when the browser is cleared, so keep a copy of anything important.

5. Quiz

  • You can join a game with a game code without registering. Pick a decent nickname: no insults and no other people's names. The host of a game may remove a player.
  • Signed-in users can create quizzes. The author is responsible for the questions and for having the right to use any images and text they add.
  • "Create with AI" writes questions with the help of language models (Google Gemini, with Groq as a spare). A model can make mistakes, so the author must check and fix the questions before playing. Only what the author types into the generator window is sent: topic, description, difficulty, number and types of questions. Do not enter personal data there.

6. Courses and certificates

  • Courses are created by instructors. The instructor status is given by a platform administrator. An instructor is responsible for the course content and for having the rights to the materials. Videos are added as YouTube links: only add what you are allowed to show.
  • Tests are graded automatically and assignments are reviewed by the instructor, who may send work back for revision.
  • A certificate is issued when the course requirements are met. It shows your full name, the course title, the date, a number and the instructor's name. You enter the name yourself, so please give your real one. A certificate with someone else's or a made-up name may be cancelled.
  • A certificate can be opened by its number or QR code so that anyone can check it is genuine. Whoever knows the number will see the name, course and date, so share the certificate only with people you choose.

7. Your content

Submissions, comments, quiz questions and course materials remain yours. By posting them on the platform you allow it to store them and show them to others as far as the site needs: for example, your post in a discussion is visible to everyone, and a course is visible to its learners.

Do not post insults, spam, advertising, other people's personal data, material you have no rights to, or anything the law forbids. Administrators may hide or remove such material.

8. What is not allowed

  • Hacking the site, looking for vulnerabilities without agreement, overloading the server, mass automated requests.
  • Getting around limits: rate limits, anti-cheating protection, typing trainer checks. Guessing other people's certificate numbers.
  • Several accounts to inflate points or rating.
  • Pretending to be an administrator, an instructor or another user.

If you find a bug or a vulnerability, please tell us. We will be grateful.

9. Results and rating

Points, rating and result tables are visible to other users. Judging is automatic. If a mistake is found in a problem or its tests, administrators may fix it and recalculate results. Results may also be removed for breaking these terms.

10. Violations and blocking

For violations administrators may warn a user, hide material, remove points, cancel a result or certificate, restrict access or block the account. If you think this was a mistake, write to us and we will look into it.
